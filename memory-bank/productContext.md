# Product Context: CosmicBalance

## User Experience
The user journey in CosmicBalance is designed to scale in complexity:
1. **System Management (Tier 1)**: Players start by managing a single star system, focusing on basic resource extraction and defending against local raiders.
2. **Galactic Exploration (Tier 2)**: Upon researching warp drives, the game expands to a galaxy of systems. Players manage trade routes and colony development.
3. **Universal Conquest (Tier 3)**: The focus shifts to macro-management of fleets across multiple galaxies. Planetary management is abstracted to keep the focus on strategic expansion.
4. **The Scourge (Tier 4)**: A late-game existential threat forces players to rethink their strategies and potentially form alliances.

## Key Features
- **Procedural Galaxy**: Every game offers a new universe with unique map topologies and resource distributions.
- **Ship Designer**: Deep customization of ship hulls, tech levels, and components (shields, weapons, engines).
- **Three-Tier Map**: Seamless zooming from universe-level clusters down to individual planets and ships.
- **P2P Multiplayer**: Direct peer-to-peer connectivity with robust **Host Migration** and **Spectator Mode** support.
- **Mobile Excellence**: UI/UX optimized for iPad/iPhone, featuring the Stellar Navigator for a premium touch experience.
- **Hybrid Economy**: Combines the raw resource trading of classic space sims with complex production pipelines.

## Design Philosophy
- **Strategic Evolution**: The player's role shifts from micro-manager to high-level strategist as the game progresses.
- **Premium RTS Aesthetic**: Vibrant space vistas, glassmorphic UI, and smooth 3D-inspired transitions.
- **Visibility-Aware UI**: The interface adapts to the current zoom level and game tier, presenting only relevant information.
- **Nostalgic modernism**: Blending the depth of 80s/90s classics (TW2002, Cosmic Balance) with modern web tech and performance.
