# Project Brief: CosmicBalance

## Core Mission
Create a deep, immersive, peer-to-peer real-time strategy (RTS) space game that combines the exploration and trading of Trade Wars 2002 with the tactical ship design and strategic planetary control of the Cosmic Balance series.

## Goals
- **Hybrid Economy**: Bridge the gap between commodity trading and industrial production.
- **Tiered Progression**: Scale gameplay from single-system management to universal conquest.
- **P2P Multiplayer**: Enable serverless multiplayer matches using PeerJS.
- **Dynamic Universe**: Procedurally generated galaxies with evolving threats like "The Scourge".
- **Tactical Depth**: Modular ship design and positional tactical combat.

## Scope
- **Phase 1**: Hybrid Economy reconciliation (Resources, Tech Tree).
- **Phase 2**: Universe & Exploration (Procedural map, 3-tier zoom).
- **Phase 3**: Tactical Combat (Ship design, Combat engine).
- **Phase 4**: AI & Networking (P2P, AI bots, The Scourge).
- **Phase 5**: Diplomacy & Alliances (Endgame mechanics).
